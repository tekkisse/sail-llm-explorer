# SAIL Data Explorer

Ask about what is in SAIL datasets, or ask a data question for your approved project.

- **Discovery:** "Does WLGP record smoking status?", "How complete is ethnicity?"
- **Data:** "How does type 2 diabetes vary by deprivation?"

Every query is shown to you before it runs. Results are aggregated, and counts below 10 are suppressed. Outputs leaving the TRE still go through the normal egress review.
